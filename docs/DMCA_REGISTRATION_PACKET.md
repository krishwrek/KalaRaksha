## 📌 Why This Protects You
Under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), any platform allowing users to upload photographs, murals, or architectural imagery is shielded from statutory copyright infringement liability (up to $150,000 per image) **IF AND ONLY IF** a Designated Agent is formally registered in the Copyright Office's public directory.

The statutory registration fee is **$6.00**, valid for 3 years.

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Filing Instructions (Takes ~5 Minutes)

1. **Visit the Official Registry:** Go to [https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/](https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/)
2. **Create Account:** Click **"Register a Service Provider"** and create an account.
3. **Fill in the Pre-Filled Form:** Copy and paste the exact details below into the respective fields.
