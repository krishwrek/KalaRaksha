# KalaRaksha: Conservation Guidelines & Restoration Charters

## Overview
Every conservation recommendation generated by **KalaRaksha** is grounded in international preservation doctrine, including:
- **ICOMOS Venice Charter (1964):** International Charter for the Conservation and Restoration of Monuments and Sites.
- **ICCROM Stone Conservation Principles:** Scientific diagnostics and remedial methods.
- **Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) Conservation Manual:** Practice standards for ancient monuments.

---

## 1. Structural Cracks & Fractures

### Priority Levels & Protocols:
- **CRITICAL (Red):** 
  - Install calibrated glass tell-tale slips or digital linear displacement sensors across major fissures to determine whether cracks are active (dynamic) or dormant.
  - Erect non-invasive structural shoring to prevent wall shearing.
  - **Prohibition:** Strictly forbid rigid Portland cement injections. Cement is chemically incompatible with historic lime/mud mortars, induces thermal stress cracking, and transfers destructive forces to adjacent stones.
  - Perform ultrasonic pulse velocity (UPV) or ground penetrating radar (GPR) before hydraulic lime grouting.
- **HIGH (Orange):**
  - Monitor crack width variations across seasonal monsoon and thermal cycles.
  - Remove micro-debris and dust using gentle dry suction.
  - Formulate natural hydraulic lime (NHL 2.0 or air-lime) grout matched in porous matrix and elasticity to historical stone.
- **MODERATE (Yellow):**
  - Establish photographic benchmark baselines.
  - Apply breathable lime pointing to prevent wind-driven rainwater ingress.

## 2. Moisture Seepage & Capillary Damp

### Priority Levels & Protocols:
- **CRITICAL (Red):**
  - Isolate the water entry point immediately: inspect roof cornices, parapet gutters, clogged gargoyles, or perimeter drainage fail points.
  - **Prohibition:** Do NOT apply impervious silicone sealers or polymer waterproofing paints. Trapping internal moisture forces water to migrate through adjacent decorative frescoes, causing explosive sub-efflorescence spalling.
  - **Prohibition:** Do NOT use forced industrial hot-air blowers; rapid drying crystallizes soluble salts violently within the stone pores.
- **HIGH (Orange):**
  - Apply sacrificial desalinization poultices (deionized water + sepiolite clay or cellulose pulp) to draw out damaging soluble salts (halite, gypsum, nitrates).
  - Construct ventilated gravel trenching (French drains) minimum 1.5m away from monument foundations to arrest capillary rising damp.
- **MODERATE (Yellow):**
  - Enhance cross-ventilation in enclosed sanctums and cave shrines to lower microclimatic relative humidity below 65%.

## 3. Peeling Paint & Delaminated Plaster

### Priority Levels & Protocols:
- **CRITICAL (Red):**
  - Apply temporary facing protection using Japanese mulberry tissue paper adhered with dilute, reversible cyclododecane or methyl cellulose.
  - Consolidate detached paint flakes using micro-syringe injection of reversible acrylic consolidants (3-5% Paraloid B-72 in acetone) or alcoholic nanolime dispersions.
  - **Prohibition:** Strictly forbid mechanical scraping, wire brushes, or adhesive tape lifts.
- **HIGH (Orange):**
  - Re-adhere hollow plaster pockets using micronized slaked lime slurry with pozzolanic micro-additives.
  - Regulate environmental conditions to minimize diurnal hygrothermal cycling.

---

## 4. Biological Growth (Moss, Algae, Lichens)

### Priority Levels & Protocols:
- **CRITICAL (Red):**
  - Treat dense active moss cushions with 2-3% quaternary ammonium biocide (benzalkonium chloride) via saturated compress.
  - Allow 72-96 hours for complete vegetation desiccation.
  - **Prohibition:** Never rip or tear live anchored moss; root-like rhizoids physically pull out mineral grains from the substrate.
- **HIGH (Orange):**
  - Remove dead, brittle biomass using soft natural-bristle (hog hair) brushes and deionized water misting.
  - Prune overarching exterior tree foliage to allow sunlight penetration and reduce boundary-layer humidity.

---

## 5. Surface Erosion & Weathering

### Priority Levels & Protocols:
- **CRITICAL (Red):**
  - Conduct high-resolution 3D laser scanning / photogrammetric archiving to preserve sub-millimeter geometric records of crumbling relief sculptures.
  - Consolidate disintegrating sandstone/limestone with breathable ethyl silicate (TEOS) treatments after salt desalinization.
  - Evaluate non-invasive, reversible protective canopies or glass shelters to shield against torrential monsoonal driving rains.
- **HIGH (Orange):**
  - Apply sacrificial breathable lime shelter-coats to absorb wind-blown sand abrasion.
  - Install minimum 1.5-meter visitor standoff barriers to prevent tactile abrasion and human sebum lipid transfer.
